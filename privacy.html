Privacy Policy

AgentLab is a private, single-user tool. It accesses only its operator's own Google account, and only at the operator's direction.

Data accessed

The operator's own files, documents and spreadsheets in Google Drive, via the Drive, Docs and Sheets APIs.

How it is used

Solely to organise the operator's own production files. Not used for advertising, not used to train machine-learning models.

Storage

An OAuth token is stored locally on the operator's own server. File content is read from Google's APIs and remains in the operator's Drive.

Sharing

No third parties. No analytics, tracking or advertising services.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Limited Use

AgentLab's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
